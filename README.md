## 👋 About Me

I'm a **Flutter mobile and frontend developer** with 1 year of experience at **BlackDevs**, building apps for Android and iOS for real clients.
I enjoy taking an idea from a **Figma design** all the way to a **Google Play release**, and making apps that stay reliable in daily use.

- 📱 Shipped **3 client Flutter apps** to Google Play (2 also on the App Store)
- ⏱️ Built timers that stay accurate after restarts, real-time clock-ins and push reminders

- 💼 **Open to full-time roles and freelance projects**
